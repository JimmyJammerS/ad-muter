# YouTube Ad Muter 🌊

Just as the current of a roaring river is transformed into energy uncontained, so can you use the nature of YouTube ads to get you back on track. 

This just mutes the ad and doesn't auto-skip or accelerate it (because I honestly don't know how to do it good and don't have enough experience yet, I guess). Instead, it gives you a quiet pause—with a soft *turin / turan* chime when Skip is ready and when your video comes back.

## How to use
1. Open YouTube.
2. Press **`F12`** (or `Ctrl + Shift + I` / `Cmd + Option + I`) to open the browser console.
3. Paste the code from `content.js` and press **Enter**.
